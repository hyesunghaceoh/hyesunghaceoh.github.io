About Me

Thank you for visiting my website! I am an Assistant Professor of Health Care Management at California State University, Long Beach. Please see the links at the top for more details on my experience and background.

I am an interdisciplinary health economist and health care management scholar. My research examines how financing policy and consolidation in health care markets affect access to care and health outcomes, with a particular focus on Medicaid and Medicare beneficiaries and historically underserved communities.

Before I decided to dedicate my career to academic research and teaching, I served as a health economics and policy consultant at the MITRE Corporation, an intern with the Shared Savings Program at the Center for Medicare, an operations assistant with Lawndale Christian Health Center, and a US Fulbright Research Grantee at Seoul National University in South Korea. Every step in my career was inspired by a deepening passion to improve the equity, quality, and accessibility of the US healthcare system.

Outside of academia, I enjoy perfecting my Korean barbeque dishes, traveling near and far, and partaking in the occasional triathlon. I started to learn how to surf and can't wait to get to know the Beach community. My values are guided by the character and teachings of Jesus, particularly the Sermon on the Mount, which inspire me to approach my work and life with purpose and compassion.
